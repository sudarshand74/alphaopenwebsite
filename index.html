2. Eligibility, health, and personal responsibility

I represent that I am at least 18 years old and legally able to enter this Agreement. AlphaOpen does not currently accept minor participants. I am physically and medically able to participate safely, or I have consulted an appropriate medical professional before participating.

  • I will inspect the court and surrounding area, use suitable equipment, follow facility rules and reasonable safety instructions, and promptly report hazards and injuries.
  • I will follow AlphaOpen’s rules and standards of sportsmanship and will not engage in violence, harassment, discrimination, threats, or intentionally unsafe conduct.

AlphaOpen does not provide medical advice, medical screening, personal coaching, transportation, security, or continuous supervision.

3. Acknowledgment and assumption of risks

I understand that tennis and related Activities involve known and unknown risks that cannot be eliminated. These include slips, trips, falls, collisions, sudden stops and changes of direction; being struck by a ball, racket, net equipment, person, or object; sprains, strains, fractures, cuts, eye injuries, concussion, cardiac events, serious injury, disability, or death; overexertion, dehydration, heat illness, cold exposure, lightning, severe weather, poor air quality, sun exposure, insects, and animals; hazardous courts and surrounding areas; defective or unsuitable equipment; theft, loss, or damage to vehicles, rackets, phones, bags, clothing, and other property; conduct, mistakes, negligence, intentional or criminal acts, or rule violations by other persons; carpools and other travel; and exposure to communicable illness.

I understand that risks may arise before, during, or after play and from scheduling, ratings, rankings, roster and lineup decisions, communications, event changes or cancellations, facility selection, volunteer supervision, emergency response, or the acts or omissions of players, spectators, facility personnel, and other third parties.

I knowingly and voluntarily accept and assume all inherent and other risks of the Activities, whether described above or not, including risks arising from the ordinary negligence of a Released Party, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

Privacy Policy

How AlphaOpen collects, uses, shares, and protects league information.

AlphaOpen.org (“AlphaOpen,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an unincorporated, volunteer-run recreational tennis association. This Policy applies to AlphaOpen.org, league registration, season administration, matches, and related communications.

1. Information we collect

We may collect names, email addresses, mobile numbers, player identifiers, shirt sizes, season-registration details, teams, roles, ranks, rosters, availability, lineups, schedules, scores, standings, awards, disciplinary and participation history, registration acceptance and electronic-signature records, communications, disputes, incident reports, and separately authorized photographs or video.

Approved operations users may sign in through Google. We may receive their Google account identifier, verified email, display name, profile image URL, and authentication status. AlphaOpen does not receive Google passwords.

Our providers may automatically process IP address, browser and device type, operating system, user agent, access time, authentication events, error and security logs, and local-storage or similar identifiers needed to operate and secure the application.

2. How we use information

We use information to register players; determine eligibility; create teams, rosters, schedules, lineups, scores, standings, playoffs, awards, and historical statistics; authenticate approved users; communicate about matches and league administration; resolve disputes; enforce rules; protect people and facilities; maintain consent, audit, backup, security, and legal records; improve the website; and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information for money or use it for third-party targeted advertising.

3. Public and participant information

We may publish or share names, player identifiers, teams, roles, ranks, rosters, lineups, schedules, match venues, scores, standings, playoff results, awards, and historical results. We do not intend to publish personal email addresses, phone numbers, authentication identifiers, private incident details, or shirt sizes. Public sports records may be copied, indexed, or retained by others.

4. How we disclose information

We may share information with volunteers who need it for league duties; players, opponents, teams, and the public as described above; technology providers including Google and Firebase; facility personnel, emergency responders, insurers, and professional advisers when reasonably necessary; authorities when legally required; and parties involved in protecting rights, safety, or league operations. Providers may process information in the United States or other locations where they operate.

5. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary. Official rosters, results, standings, awards, player identifiers, and participation history may be retained long term. Consent and incident records may be retained through the applicable limitation period and any related claim. Private contact, authentication, administrative, and technical information should be deleted or de-identified when no longer needed, although residual copies may temporarily remain in protected backups.

6. Security

AlphaOpen uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a volunteer-run league, including role-based access and authenticated administrative tools. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and AlphaOpen cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or misuse will never occur.

7. Your choices and requests

You may ask what information we maintain about you, request corrections, request deletion where retention is not required or reasonably permitted, withdraw optional publicity permission for future uses, or submit a privacy complaint. We may verify your identity. Accurate official sports, disciplinary, consent, incident, and legal records may be retained when reasonably necessary or permitted by law.

8. Communications

We may use contact information for registration, scheduling, safety, scores, accounts, rules, and administrative communications. Optional promotional messages will use separate consent where required and include an opt-out method. Opting out of promotions does not stop essential league or safety communications.

9. Adults only

Registration and participation are limited to adults age 18 and older. A person under 18 may not register, participate, or provide personal information through the registration process. AlphaOpen.org is not directed to children under 13, and AlphaOpen does not knowingly collect personal information online directly from a child under 13.

10. Third parties and policy changes

Links to Google, Facebook, facilities, maps, and other third parties are governed by their own terms and privacy practices. We may update this Policy when league practices, technology, vendors, or law change. We will post the revised version with a new date and provide additional notice or seek consent when required.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, email AlphaOpenEC@gmail.com.
