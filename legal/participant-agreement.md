# AlphaOpen Participant Agreement, Assumption of Risk, Release and Waiver

Effective date: July 29, 2026
Organization: AlphaOpen.org, an unincorporated, volunteer-run recreational tennis association (“AlphaOpen”)

**PLEASE READ CAREFULLY. THIS AGREEMENT AFFECTS LEGAL RIGHTS, INCLUDING THE RIGHT TO SUE.**

## 1. Voluntary recreational participation

I voluntarily request to register for, be rostered in, attend, practice for, volunteer with, or participate in the AlphaOpen recreational tennis league and its related matches, practices, playoffs, meetings, social events, communications, and activities (collectively, the “Activities”). I understand that AlphaOpen is organized and administered primarily by unpaid volunteers. Players also participate voluntarily. AlphaOpen does not employ, train, supervise, certify, or warrant the skill, fitness, conduct, or safety of players, captains, officials, volunteers, spectators, or facility personnel.

Participation is a privilege, not a right. AlphaOpen may establish eligibility requirements, make scheduling and ranking decisions, enforce league rules, discipline conduct, suspend or remove a participant, cancel or relocate an Activity, or modify a season when reasonably necessary.

## 2. Eligibility, health, and personal responsibility

I represent that:

- I am at least 18 years old and legally able to enter this Agreement. AlphaOpen does not currently accept minor participants.
- I am physically and medically able to participate safely, or I have consulted an appropriate medical professional before participating.
- I will not participate while impaired or when illness, injury, weather, court conditions, equipment, or another circumstance makes participation unsafe.
- I am responsible for my own health decisions, hydration, medication, protective equipment, transportation, personal property, and medical costs.
- I will inspect the court and surrounding area, use equipment in suitable condition, follow posted facility rules and reasonable safety instructions, and promptly report hazards and injuries.
- I will follow AlphaOpen's rules and standards of sportsmanship and will not engage in violence, harassment, discrimination, threats, or intentionally unsafe conduct.

AlphaOpen does not provide medical advice, medical screening, personal coaching, transportation, security, or continuous supervision.

## 3. Acknowledgment and assumption of risks

I understand that tennis and related Activities involve known and unknown risks that cannot be eliminated. These include, without limitation:

- slips, trips, falls, collisions, sudden stops and changes of direction;
- being struck by a ball, racket, net equipment, person, or other object;
- sprains, strains, fractures, cuts, eye injuries, concussion, cardiac events, other serious injury, disability, or death;
- overexertion, dehydration, heat illness, cold exposure, lightning, severe weather, poor air quality, sun exposure, insects, and animals;
- wet, cracked, uneven, poorly lit, crowded, or otherwise hazardous courts and surrounding areas;
- defective, misused, or unsuitable equipment;
- conduct, mistakes, negligence, or rule violations by players, spectators, volunteers, captains, officials, facility owners or operators, and other persons;
- travel to and from Activities, including transportation arranged independently by participants; and
- exposure to communicable illness.

I knowingly and voluntarily accept and assume all inherent and other risks of the Activities, whether described above or not, including risks arising from the ordinary negligence of a Released Party, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

## 4. Release and waiver of liability

In consideration for being allowed to participate, I, for myself and for my heirs, estate, personal representatives, guardians, successors, and assigns, release and forever discharge the following persons and entities (collectively, the “Released Parties”):

- AlphaOpen.org and the unincorporated AlphaOpen recreational tennis association;
- its current and former executive committee members, officers, directors, captains, coordinators, administrators, neutral approvers, scorekeepers, officials, agents, and other volunteers;
- other participating players and teams; and
- to the extent they are intended third-party beneficiaries of this Agreement, the owners, operators, managers, sponsors, and licensors of facilities used for an Activity.

This release applies to claims, demands, causes of action, damages, losses, costs, or expenses for bodily injury, illness, disability, death, property damage, or other loss arising out of or relating to an Activity, including a claim alleging the **ordinary negligence** of a Released Party, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

This Agreement does not release liability that applicable law does not permit to be released, including liability finally determined to result from gross negligence, reckless or willful misconduct, or intentional harm.

## 5. Covenant not to sue and indemnity

To the fullest extent permitted by law, I agree not to bring or maintain a claim covered by the release above. I will indemnify and hold the Released Parties harmless from third-party claims, losses, or reasonable costs, including reasonable attorneys' fees, caused by my intentional misconduct, violation of league or facility rules, or negligent acts or omissions.

This indemnity is not intended to require me to indemnify a Released Party for that Released Party's gross negligence, reckless or willful misconduct, intentional harm, or any liability that cannot lawfully be shifted.

## 6. Medical emergencies

If I appear to need urgent care and I am unable to act, I authorize AlphaOpen volunteers to contact emergency services and to share reasonably necessary identification, emergency-contact, and health information with responders. I understand that volunteers are not required to provide first aid, transportation, or medical care and may not be medically trained.

I am responsible for all medical, ambulance, transportation, and related expenses incurred for me. This authorization does not create a duty for AlphaOpen or any volunteer to diagnose, treat, supervise, or guarantee my safety.

## 7. League records and public results

I understand that AlphaOpen may record and publish information reasonably necessary to operate and document a recreational sports league, including my name, player identifier, team, captain or volunteer role, rank, lineup, schedule, match result, score, standings, awards, and historical participation. AlphaOpen will handle contact information and other nonpublic information as described in its Privacy Policy.

Publicity photographs, video, testimonials, or promotional use of my likeness require the separate optional permission presented during registration. Declining that optional permission will not prevent ordinary league participation, although incidental appearance in crowd or event photographs may not always be avoidable.

AlphaOpen does not warrant the condition, availability, accessibility, lighting, security, staffing, or fitness of a facility. Schedules, rosters, rankings, results, and website information may contain errors or change without notice.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Activities and website are provided “as is” and “as available,” without warranties of any kind.

## 9. Rules, discipline, and disputes

I will first submit league-administration disputes through AlphaOpen's published rules and Executive Committee procedures. This requirement does not prevent a person from seeking emergency assistance, reporting unlawful conduct, cooperating with authorities, or exercising a right that cannot legally be limited.
