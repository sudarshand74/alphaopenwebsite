# AlphaOpen Legal Package — Implementation Notes

Status: Completed draft for Georgia attorney review
Prepared: July 29, 2026

This folder contains:

- `participant-agreement.md` — the agreement every player should affirmatively accept before being placed on a roster or participating.
- `privacy-policy.md` — the public notice describing AlphaOpen's actual data practices.
- `website-disclaimer.md` — a short website-use disclaimer suitable for a footer link.
- `registration-consent.md` — exact consent language and the acceptance record AlphaOpen should retain.

## Important limitations

These are practical U.S.-oriented templates, not legal advice and not a guarantee against liability. A disclaimer cannot eliminate every legal duty. Enforceability depends on the governing state, the participant's age, how consent is obtained, the clarity and prominence of the waiver, and the facts of a claim.

Before publication, a licensed attorney in the state where AlphaOpen operates and matches are played should:

1. Confirm that describing AlphaOpen.org as an “unincorporated, volunteer-run recreational tennis association” is accurate and advise whether it should formally organize as a Georgia nonprofit corporation or nonprofit unincorporated association.
2. Review the release of ordinary negligence, indemnity, medical authorization, venue release, governing-law, and forum provisions.
3. Confirm whether a parent may waive a minor's claims and prepare a separate minor-participant form if needed.
4. Review insurance coverage. A waiver is not a substitute for general liability, directors-and-officers, accident/participant, cyber, and any required workers' compensation or auto coverage.
5. Confirm whether AlphaOpen's collection of information from residents of any particular state triggers additional privacy notices or rights.

## Recommended consent flow

Do not rely only on “By registering, you agree” in small print or only on a footer link. Before accepting a registration or roster placement:

1. Show links to the Participant Agreement and Privacy Policy next to the consent control.
2. Require an unchecked checkbox with the text in `registration-consent.md`.
3. Require the player to enter their full legal name as an electronic signature.
4. Prevent submission unless both fields are completed.
5. Provide a download/print option and email or otherwise make a copy available.
6. Store an immutable consent record containing the fields listed in `registration-consent.md`.
7. Require renewed acceptance whenever a material term changes and preferably at the start of each season.
8. Use a separate, optional checkbox for publicity/photo permission. Do not bundle it into the required liability waiver.

The current AlphaOpen site says there is no public registration and regular players do not need accounts. Therefore, adding terms only to Google sign-in would cover operations users but not all players. Consent must be collected during the actual player intake or season-roster process, including imported players, before they participate.

## Operational protections beyond documents

- Adopt written lightning, extreme-heat, air-quality, unsafe-court, injury, and emergency-action procedures.
- Give captains a short safety checklist and document training.
- Maintain incident reports and preserve relevant communications after an injury.
- Require venue permission and comply with each facility's rules.
- Avoid representing volunteers as medical, legal, officiating, or safety professionals.
- Collect only information that is needed, restrict administrative access, use multifactor authentication, and establish a retention/deletion schedule.
- Keep commercial/promotional email consent separate from necessary league communications.
- Review the package annually and whenever practices, vendors, or laws change.
