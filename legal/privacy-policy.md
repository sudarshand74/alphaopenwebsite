# AlphaOpen Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026
Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AlphaOpen.org (“AlphaOpen,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), an unincorporated, volunteer-run recreational tennis association, collects, uses, discloses, and protects information through AlphaOpen.org, league registration, season administration, matches, and related communications.

## 1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with AlphaOpen, we may collect:

### Information you or a league administrator provides

- name, email address, mobile number, and player identifier;
- shirt size and other season-registration details;
- team, captain or volunteer role, rank, roster position, eligibility, availability, lineup, schedule, score, standing, award, disciplinary, waiver, and participation history;
- registration acceptance, electronic signature, agreement version, acceptance date and time, and related audit information;
- communications, questions, complaints, incident reports, and rule or score disputes;
- emergency-contact or health information if AlphaOpen specifically requests it for safety purposes; and
- photographs, video, or testimonials when submitted or separately authorized.

Please do not send sensitive health, financial, government-identification, or other information that AlphaOpen has not requested.

### Google sign-in information

Approved captains, Executive Committee members, approvers, and administrators may sign in through Google. We may receive and store their Google account identifier, verified email address, display name, profile image URL, and authentication status. AlphaOpen does not receive the user's Google password.

### Technical information

Our hosting, authentication, database, security, and application providers may automatically process information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, user agent, approximate time of access, authentication events, error and security logs, and local-storage or similar identifiers needed to operate, secure, and remember the application.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- register players and determine league and season eligibility;
- create teams, ranks, rosters, schedules, lineups, match records, scores, standings, playoffs, awards, and historical statistics;
- authenticate and authorize approved operations users;
- communicate about matches, deadlines, safety, rule changes, incidents, disputes, and league administration;
- respond to requests and correct records;
- enforce league rules and protect participants, facilities, AlphaOpen, and the public;
- maintain consent, security, audit, backup, and legal records;
- troubleshoot, secure, evaluate, and improve the website and league operations; and
- comply with law and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not use personal information for third-party targeted advertising.

## 3. Information visible to the public or league participants

AlphaOpen is a competitive league whose operation requires sharing some participation information. We may publish or share names, player identifiers, teams, captain or volunteer roles, ranks, rosters, lineups, schedules, match venues, scores, standings, playoff results, awards, and historical results on AlphaOpen.org or in league communications.

We do not intend to publish personal email addresses, phone numbers, authentication identifiers, private incident details, or shirt sizes. Players should understand that public sports records may be copied, indexed, or retained by others after AlphaOpen changes or removes them.

## 4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information:

- to Executive Committee members, captains, approvers, administrators, and other volunteers who need it for league duties;
- to players, opponents, teams, and the public as described above;
- to technology and service providers that host, authenticate, store, secure, support, or communicate for the league, including Google and Firebase services;
- to facility personnel, emergency responders, insurers, professional advisers, or authorities when reasonably necessary for safety, an incident, insurance, or legal compliance;
- when required by subpoena, court order, law, or a valid governmental request;
- when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, security problems, misconduct, or threats to rights or safety; and
- in connection with a reorganization or transfer of league operations, subject to appropriate protection.

Service providers may process information in the United States or other locations where they or their subprocessors operate.

## 5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. Season rosters, scores, standings, awards, player identifiers, and participation history may be kept long term as official league records. Consent and incident records may be retained through the applicable limitation period and any related claim.

Private contact, authentication, administrative, and technical information should be deleted or de-identified when it is no longer needed for league operations, safety, security, backup, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Residual copies may remain temporarily in protected backups.

## 6. Security

AlphaOpen uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a volunteer-run league, including role-based access and authenticated administrative tools. Our application currently uses Firebase services, which state that supported services encrypt data in transit and that Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication encrypt data at rest.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. AlphaOpen cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or misuse will never occur. If you believe information has been compromised, contact us promptly.

## 7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to:

- ask what information AlphaOpen maintains about you;
- request correction of inaccurate contact or profile information;
- request deletion of information that AlphaOpen is not required or reasonably permitted to retain;
- withdraw optional photo or promotional permission for future uses; or
- ask a privacy question or make a complaint.

We may need to verify your identity. Deletion or restriction may affect eligibility or our ability to operate your participation. We may retain and continue to publish accurate official sports, disciplinary, consent, incident, and legal records when reasonably necessary or permitted by law.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights. AlphaOpen will respond to verified requests as required by applicable law.

## 8. League communications

AlphaOpen may use email or phone information for necessary registration, scheduling, safety, score, account, rule, and administrative communications. Any optional promotional or fundraising messages will use separate consent where required and will include a method to opt out. Opting out of promotional messages does not stop essential league or safety communications.

## 9. Children and minors

AlphaOpen registration and participation are limited to adults who are at least 18 years old. A person under 18 may not register, participate, or provide personal information through the registration process.

AlphaOpen.org is not directed to children under 13, and AlphaOpen does not knowingly collect personal information online directly from a child under 13. If you believe a child has provided information without required parental authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 10. Third-party websites and facilities

AlphaOpen.org may link to Google, Facebook, facilities, maps, or other third parties. Their services and privacy practices are governed by their own terms, not this Policy. A link does not imply that AlphaOpen controls or endorses the third party.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when league practices, technology, vendors, or law change. We will post the revised version with a new “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects how previously collected information is used, we will provide additional notice or seek consent when required.
